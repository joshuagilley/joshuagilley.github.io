About Me

I'm a software engineer who cares deeply about well-designed systems, accessible pixel-perfect interfaces, and clean, maintainable architecture. I'm drawn to problems where thoughtful design and strong engineering intersect.

At Bear Cognition, I develop data-driven logistics applications, including Outlook add-ins that give users clear visibility into shipments and offers so they can place and win bids. My work spans React, LangGraph, Python, and MongoDB.
